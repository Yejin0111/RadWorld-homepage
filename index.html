Foundation pretraining

A shared generative foundation for 3D CT and MRI.

The human body.
A shared generative foundation.

RadWorld learns a shared generative distribution of how the human body appears in 3D CT and MRI, across anatomical and pathological states. Task-specific adaptation enables simulation under different imaging and clinical conditions.

Generation

Generate virtual 3D CT and MRI volumes.

A shared paradigm for scan generation, report-to-volume generation and mask-to-volume generation.
